  • parser: the shared parser reads every date month first. Our tenants write the day first, so day and month swap. This is the fix that #4471 queued.
  • hint: the booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People type what the hint says, and the form reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. The parser fix does not touch the form.
  • shift: the invite lands one day early or late. The day and month do not swap. Reporters in two time zones get different days, and an invite can arrive before the booking. This is a time zone bug. The desk read these reports as one-day moves (desk-notes.json), but the table on #4471 counts them under parser.
